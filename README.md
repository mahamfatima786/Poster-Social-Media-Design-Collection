# Poster & Social Media Design Collection

This repository contains 5 creative poster and social media design projects.  
Each project includes posters, Instagram posts, and story designs with a unique visual style.

---

## Projects Included

### 1. Music Festival Posters
Bold and energetic designs for a music festival.  
Includes event posters, promotional Instagram posts, and stories.

**Style:** Vibrant, modern, high-energy  


---

### 2. Motivational Quote Series
Clean and impactful typography-based designs.  
A series of motivational posters and social media content focused on mindset and growth.

**Style:** Minimal, typography-focused, elegant  


---

### 3. Food / Restaurant Promotion
Appetizing and colorful designs for food promotion.  
Includes menu posters, food posts, and promotional stories.

**Style:** Fresh, colorful, mouth-watering  


---

### 4. Fashion / Clothing Brand Campaign
Trendy and aesthetic designs for a fashion brand launch.  
Includes campaign posters, lookbook-style posts, and stories.

**Style:** Modern, stylish, lifestyle-oriented  


---

### 5. Awareness Campaign
Impactful designs focused on social awareness topics.  
Includes posters and social media content for meaningful causes.

**Style:** Emotional, clean, powerful  


---

## Design Approach

- Strong visual hierarchy
- Consistent branding within each project
- Optimized for both print and social media
- Modern layouts with clean composition
- Focus on storytelling through design

---

## Tools Used

- Adobe Photoshop / Illustrator / Figma

- `stories/` → Instagram/Facebook story designs
